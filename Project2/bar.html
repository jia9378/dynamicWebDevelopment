BARS
TAYĒR AND ELEMENTARY
Located in Old Street, London, Tayer + Elementary is one of the world’s most innovative and prestigious bars. Founded by Monica Berg and Alex Kratena in 2019, this bar has been acclaimed by critics and customers alike, which secures him a place in the top 10 of the 50 Best Bars list, including 2nd place in 2022.
THE CONNAUGHT BAR
The Connaught Bar is a luxurious cocktail bar located in the heart of Mayfair, London. Known for its elegant design and exceptional service, it has been awarded numerous accolades, including the title of Best Bar in the World. The bar offers a unique menu of classic and innovative cocktails, all crafted with the finest ingredients.
SCARFES BAR
Tucked inside the super-luxe Rosewood Hotel and named after the legendary British artist and caricaturist Gerald Scarfe, this is a perfect mix of a classic lounge bar and eccentric drinking den. Scarfe’s own collection of surreal paintings decorates the marble walls, transforming the bar into an art gallery.
THE SEED LIBRARY
The latest from the Mr Lyan team behind the award-winning Lyaness, Seed Library takes a new, back-to-basics approach. Located in the basement of the One Hundred Shoreditch hotel, the space has been created to be as comfortable as possible, with soothing, warm tones that takes more than a little inspiration from the 1970s.
KWĀNT MAYFAIR
Kwānt 2.0 brings much of the original Kwānt’s charms – Erik Lorincz’s madcap creativity; artful interiors; immaculately white-jacketed bartenders; dazzlingly surprising drinks – to London's Stratton Street, an olive stone’s throw from the Ritz (and from the bar's former home on Heddon Street). Ceiling fans, palms, chess-board-topped tables, yolk-yellow bouclé furnishings and a striking steel bar provide retro sartorial swagger.
SWIFT
Opened in 2016, Swift has become a favourite nesting spot for discerning drinkers in Soho – and a recent entry into The World’s 50 Best Bars. Run by Mia Johansson and Bobby Hiddleston (of Milk & Honey fame), with Nightjar and Oriole owners Rosie Stimpson and Edmund Weil in support, the combined wealth of industry experience is evident at every turn. A bright, ground-floor bar offers sunny pours, filled brioche rolls and get-them-before-they’re-gone oysters, while downstairs is all dark booths and dark spirits, with more than 250 whiskies behind the bar.
